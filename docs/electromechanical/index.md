# Electromechanical Engineering

Electromechanical Engineering combines mechanics, manufacturing, energy systems, electrical engineering and control systems. Students learn how to design, analyse, automate and optimize machines, products and industrial processes.

## Career Paths

Graduates can be found in a wide range of technical and industrial sectors:

* Mechanical Design Engineering
* Mechatronics Engineering
* Robotics and Automation
* Manufacturing and Production Engineering
* Industrial Process Engineering
* Energy Systems and Sustainability
* Automotive Engineering
* Aerospace Engineering
* Medical Devices and Clinical Engineering
* Research and Development
* Quality Engineering
* Technical Consulting
* Product Development
* Operations and Technical Management
* Entrepreneurship and Technology Start-ups

Typical employers include manufacturing companies, energy providers, aerospace firms, automotive companies, robotics and automation integrators, engineering consultancies, research centres and high-tech product developers. Graduates may also continue towards doctoral research.
