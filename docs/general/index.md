# General Engineering

The General Engineering programme provides the scientific, mathematical and technological foundations that underpin all engineering disciplines. Students develop analytical thinking, quantitative problem-solving skills and a broad understanding of engineering principles before choosing a specialization.

## Career Paths

The General Engineering programme serves as a foundation for further specialization in:

* Electromechanical Engineering
* Electronics and ICT Engineering
* Chemical Engineering
* Biochemical Engineering

Students develop competencies that are broadly applicable across engineering disciplines, including:

* Mathematical Modelling
* Data Analysis
* Programming
* Experimental Methods
* Engineering Design
* Scientific Problem Solving
* Technical Communication
* Project Work

In the second year of the bachelor programme, students choose a specialization. The broad scientific and engineering foundation also prepares graduates for interdisciplinary projects and emerging technological fields.
