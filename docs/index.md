# KU Leuven Industrial Engineering Wiki

!!! note "Site in construction"

    This site is still in construction, information may be lacking or incomplete.


A student-maintained knowledge base for the Engineering Technology programmes at KU Leuven.

This wiki aims to collect study advice, course information, summaries, exam experiences, useful resources and practical guidance from students and alumni.

## What You Can Find Here

### Course Pages

Each course has its own page containing:

* Course information
* Study advice

* Useful resources
* Software recommendations
* Student reviews
* Frequently asked questions

### Files and Resources

For each course you will find dedicated folders for:

* Past Exams
* Lab Material
* Exercise Sessions
* Summaries
* Formula Sheets
* Solved Exercises

### Programme Overviews

Each programme contains:

* Curriculum structure
* Bachelor and Master courses
* Specialization options
* Career opportunities

## Contributing

This project is maintained by students and alumni.

You can contribute by:

* Improving course pages
* Adding study advice
* Sharing exam experiences
* Uploading summaries and formula sheets
* Reporting outdated information
* Fixing mistakes

Use the "Edit this page" button available throughout the wiki to submit improvements.

---

## Guidelines

### Recommended Contributions

* Personal summaries
* Formula sheets
* Study guides
* Solved exercises
* Exam preparation advice
* Software tutorials
* Project experiences

### Please Avoid Uploading

* Copyrighted textbooks
* Paid course material
* Restricted university content
* Material that violates KU Leuven policies
