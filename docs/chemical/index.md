# Chemical Engineering

Chemical Engineering applies chemistry, physics and mathematics to the design, optimization and operation of industrial processes. The programme combines fundamental sciences with process engineering, sustainability and industrial applications.

## Career Paths

Graduates work in industries where chemical, biological and process technologies play a central role:

* Process Engineering
* Chemical Plant Design and Operation
* Pharmaceutical Engineering
* Biotechnology
* Food Engineering
* Environmental Engineering
* Sustainable Energy and Circular Economy
* Materials Engineering
* Polymer and Plastics Industry
* Metallurgy and Advanced Materials
* Water and Waste Treatment
* Process Safety Engineering
* Quality Assurance and Validation
* Research and Development
* Technical Consulting
* Product Development
* Industrial Management
* Entrepreneurship and Technology Start-ups

Typical employers include chemical companies, pharmaceutical firms, biotechnology companies, food producers, environmental consultancies, materials manufacturers, energy companies, research institutes and governmental agencies. Graduates may also continue towards doctoral research.
